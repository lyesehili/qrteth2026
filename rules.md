# QRT x ETH Hackathon — 24/11/2026

Competition: https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/qrt-x-eth-hackathon-24-11-2026/overview

## Objective: Predict the Last 30 Minutes of Trading

At the end of each trading day, many investors trade at the close, and index funds rebalance. Prices can continue the move of the day or reverse it.

The objective is to predict which stocks will outperform their country market during the last 30 minutes of trading.

The dataset contains intraday prices of 386 European stocks, sampled every 5 minutes, from the market open to 30 minutes before the close.

For each stock-day, predict the return from the cutoff to the market close, relative to the other stocks of the same country.

Only the sign of the prediction matters, and larger target movements receive higher weights.

All test days are after all training days.

## Task

Each row represents one stock i on one trading day d.

Trading session:

    Market open → Observed prices → Cutoff → Hidden 30 minutes → Market close

The cutoff is 30 minutes before the market close.

Prices from the market open to the cutoff are observable. Prices during the final 30 minutes are hidden.

## Input

`Price TS` contains one price every 5 minutes. Element k corresponds to the price at the end of the kth 5-minute bar.

The last element is the price at the cutoff.

Missing prices are represented by null values.

A full observed session contains 96 prices.

The absolute price level is arbitrary. Only ratios of prices should be used.

## Target

Raw close return:

    raw_return[i,d] = P_close[i,d] / P_cutoff[i,d] - 1

The target is the raw close return minus its mean across all stocks belonging to the same country on the same trading day:

    y[i,d] = raw_return[i,d] - mean(raw_return[j,d] for j in same country)

A positive target means that the stock outperformed its country during the last 30 minutes of trading.

## Evaluation metric

The score is the weighted accuracy of the predicted sign:

    score = sum(abs(y_true) * (y_true * y_pred > 0)) / sum(abs(y_true))

Properties:

- The score is between 0 and 1.
- Higher is better.
- Random signs produce a score of approximately 0.5.
- Only the sign of the prediction matters.
- The magnitude of the prediction has no effect.
- A prediction of exactly zero is always incorrect.
- A target of exactly zero has no weight.
- Larger absolute target returns contribute more to the final score.

## Official benchmark

Predict the negative of the last observed 5-minute return:

    y_pred = -(P[-1] / P[-2] - 1)

The official benchmark achieves a cross-validation score of 0.523 using four folds split by `DATE_ID`.

## Dataset

The dataset contains 386 European stocks from six countries:

- Germany (DEU)
- Spain (ESP)
- France (FRA)
- United Kingdom (GBR)
- Italy (ITA)
- Netherlands (NLD)

Prices are synthetic, generated by a generative model trained on real intraday market data.

Stocks and dates are anonymized.

All test days occur after all training days.

### Columns in x_train.parquet and x_test.parquet

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| ID | Unique stock-day identifier linking features, targets and predictions |
| DATE_ID | Anonymous trading day identifier. Its order does not follow the calendar |
| STOCK_ID | Anonymous stock identifier, consistent across training and test sets |
| COUNTRY | Listing country: DEU, ESP, FRA, GBR, ITA or NLD |
| OPEN_TIME | Market opening time in UTC, HH:MM |
| CLOSE_TIME | Market closing time in UTC, HH:MM |
| Price TS | Intraday prices sampled every 5 minutes, from market open to cutoff |

Important dataset properties:

- Each row corresponds to one stock-day.
- Multiple stocks share the same DATE_ID.
- STOCK_ID is consistent between training and test sets.
- DATE_ID values are anonymized and their numerical order does not represent chronological order.
- Missing prices are represented by null values.
- A full observed session contains 96 prices.
- Absolute price levels are arbitrary; use price ratios.
- Test days are chronologically after training days.

### Columns in y_train.csv

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| ID | Unique stock-day identifier |
| Close return | Country-demeaned return from cutoff to market close |

## Submission requirements

The submission must be a CSV file containing exactly 219,157 data rows and two columns:

    ID,Close return

The submission includes IDs from both training and test datasets:

- Training IDs: 0 to 144224 inclusive.
- Test IDs: 144225 to 219156 inclusive.

Example:

    ID,Close return
    0,0.0012
    1,-0.0004

Kaggle rejects submissions if:

- The `Close return` column is missing.
- Predictions are not numeric.
- An ID appears more than once.
- An ID has no prediction.
- Predictions contain NaN or infinity.

Predictions of exactly zero are valid but always incorrect under the scoring metric.

The provided sample submission contains only zeros and therefore receives a score of zero.

The public leaderboard scoring subset is not specified in the competition description.

The private leaderboard scores the test days and determines the final ranking.

The final submission must be uploaded before the hackathon deadline.
